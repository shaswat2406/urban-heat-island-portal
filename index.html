Policy Matrix & Urban Action Blueprint

Comparative analysis of India's statutory cooling codes, prescriptive Solar Reflectance Index (SRI) mandates, and urban local body bylaws.

National Strategy MoEF&CC 2019

India Cooling Action Plan (ICAP)

First-of-its-kind national blueprint aiming to curb cooling demand by 20–25% and refrigerants by 25–30% by 2037–38 through active building envelope passive strategies.

Target: Mandatory cool roof bylaws across all tier-1 & tier-2 municipal corporations.
Prescriptive Code BEE (MoP)

BEE ECBC 2017 Standards

Statutory envelope efficiency norms for commercial facilities with connected loads ≥ 100 kW. Dictates strict roof solar reflectance index thresholds.

Thresholds: Low-slope roof SRI ≥ 78 • Steep-slope roof SRI ≥ 29.
Pioneering State Law MA&UD 2023

Telangana Cool Roof Policy

India's first statutory mandatory cool roof directive. Mandates cool roofs for all non-residential buildings and residential plots ≥ 600 sq. yards.

Benchmark: 300 sq. km target by 2028 across Hyderabad & state urban local bodies.
